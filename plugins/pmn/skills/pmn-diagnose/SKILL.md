# Diagnosing a case with PMN

A diagnosis without evidence is an opinion with PMN's vocabulary. PMN supplies the questions and the tests; the
facts come from the user, from sources, or are clearly marked as assumptions. The value of the output is that
someone can check it: every stage call has evidence, every claim about PMN has a section id.

## Where the text is

This skill carries the method, not the manuscript. Work from the text of PMN v126, never from memory: PMN is
recent and specific, and a model's memory of it produces generic materialism with PMN's words pasted on and section
numbers that do not exist.

1. **The `pmn` skill, if installed.** Its files sit next to this skill's folder: `../pmn/references/`
   (`index.md` lists every section, `glossary.md` the defined terms, `sections/<id>.txt` the full text of one
   section). If that path is missing, look among the installed skills for a `pmn` folder with `references/index.md`.
2. **Otherwise the website.** https://novadharma-hub.github.io/pmn-framework/txt/index.txt lists every section with the URL of its own plain-text file.
3. **Otherwise ask.** Ask the user to paste the sections named below, or to install the `pmn` skill
   (https://novadharma-hub.github.io/pmn-framework/#/guide/install). Say plainly that you cannot check the text until then.

Open every section you rely on and read it in full before citing it. Cite section ids (§7.3c-i) and quote the
sentence behind each key claim.

## Sections to read for this work

- §7.3c-i: the five-stage capture sequence. §7.3b: early-warning indicators for each stage.
- §6.2 and §6.3: power as structural position; judge by incentives and position, not stated mission.
- §12.1b: second-order effects across layers, including the effects of doing nothing.
- §12.5: failure modes, including that capture diagnostics apply regardless of ideology.
- §12.5d: misuse triggers, to run on your own draft. §12.5e: how to proceed with partial data.
- §6.6 when the case touches legitimacy (§7.3b leans on its leading and lagging indicators).
- §7.3d when the user asks for remedies. §13.1c when the conflict is long-standing and someone calls it permanent.

This list is where to start, not a boundary: when a section you read points to another that bears on the case,
open that one too.

## Procedure

1. **Scope.** Name the institution, its stated mandate, the period, and the populations affected. If the facts are
   thin, ask for them or list what you are assuming. Keep three kinds of statement apart: facts the user gave,
   facts from your general knowledge (flag these, and note they may be out of date), and inferences.
2. **Material arrangement.** Who funds it, who decides, who bears the costs, who gains. PMN requires the
   distribution to be stated (§12.3b, the distribution visibility test), so do not stop at "stakeholders".
3. **Capture stage.** For each of the five stages, list the evidence for and against, using the §7.3b indicators.
   The case sits at the highest stage with real evidence; say how confident you are. Apply the same tests whatever
   the institution's ideology and whatever you or the user think of it (§12.5).
4. **Consequences.** First-order and second-order, across the layers in §12.1b, and the consequences of inaction.
5. **Check your own draft (§12.5d).** Does the conclusion conveniently favour the most powerful actor? Is the
   suffering of the least visible population missing? Did the user's framing already contain the conclusion? If a
   trigger fires, say so and adjust.
6. **Data gaps (§12.5e).** Name what is missing and why it might be missing. Give the strength of each inference
   in the form "supported at [strength] given [assumptions]; reversed by [evidence]".
7. **Remedies, only if asked.** Match §7.3d's design principles to the stage: early stages can be interrupted;
   from stage three, internal reform is badly impaired (§7.3b).

## Rules

- Do not overclaim. "Stage 4 indicators present" needs the indicators; if the evidence stops at stage 2, say so
  even if the case feels worse.
- On live political controversies, give the structure and the evidence, not a partisan verdict. PMN's tests cut
  both ways; show where they cut.
- If the text is silent on something the case needs, say so rather than inventing a PMN position.

## Output

1. **Diagnosis:** one paragraph, plain language.
2. **Facts used:** given / general knowledge / assumed.
3. **Capture stage:** a table with columns Stage, Evidence, Status (present / absent / unknown).
4. **Who pays, who gains.**
5. **Second-order effects,** including of inaction.
6. **Confidence and what would overturn it:** specific, observable evidence.
7. **Sections read.**

Answer in the user's language; keep section ids and quoted sentences in the original English.
