# Known inconsistencies in PMN {{VERSION}}

Places where the manuscript disagrees with itself, found while building and testing these skills. The manuscript is
being revised, so this list will change. Each entry gives the recommended reading; say which reading you use.

## 1. G has two meanings in the main formula

- §15.4: "The G variable (intergenerational transmission) is analytically distinct from duration..."
- §15.4, later: "The governing coalition's primary interest-maintenance strategy is to ensure that G remains low —
  that the populations bearing structural suffering understand themselves as having separate rather than shared
  interests." That is cross-group alignment, which §15.4 heads "Cross-group alignment (Ga)".
- §15.2 writes the formula as A = S × D × P × G with "all four variables".

**Reading:** G = intergenerational transmission, as explicitly defined. Treat cross-group alignment as a separate
factor, Ga, and name it apart. If the analysis depends on coalition formation, discuss Ga explicitly rather than
folding it into G.

## 2. P is named two ways

- §15.4 heading: "Geographic spread (P)"; the paragraph under it: "The population scale variable P reflects a
  non-linear relationship between the number of people bearing structural suffering and the political
  consequences".
- §15.2 and §15.4 elsewhere call the same factor "Scale": "the proportion of a relevant population experiencing the
  suffering".

**Reading:** P = scale, the proportion of the relevant population affected. Treat geographic spread as one aspect of
scale, not a separate measure.

## 3. T and A are used for the same quantity

- §15.2: "System Transformation Pressure (T) = A = S × D × P × G [where A is Accumulated Pressure as defined in
  15.4; the shorthand T = S × D × P in some cross-references omits G for legibility but G is always implied."

**Reading:** T (transformation pressure) and A (accumulated pressure) are the same product. Where a passage writes
T = S × D × P, G is still implied.

## 4. The "canonical" definitions leave out D, P and G

- §15.0b is titled "Canonical Term Definitions", but it defines S, R, B, V, W, Ef and E, not D, P or G.

**Reading:** take D, P and G from §15.4 and cite §15.4 for them.

## 5. "Advanced V" has no independent indicator

- §15.0b: "Advanced V is internalised suppression ... in which institutional arrangements successfully induce the
  populations bearing structural suffering to validate that suffering as natural, deserved, or individually
  caused."
- Nothing in the text gives a way to detect advanced V other than the absence of complaint it is meant to explain.
  So "people do not complain" fits both "little structural suffering" and "suffering hidden by advanced V".

**Reading:** when you infer advanced V, name the evidence for it that does not depend on the lack of complaint
(material indicators of suffering, suppressed channels, what people say in safe settings). Without such evidence,
say the inference cannot be tested in this case.
