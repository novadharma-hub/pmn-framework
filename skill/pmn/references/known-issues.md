# Known inconsistencies in PMN {{VERSION}}

The manuscript is being revised. This list is deliberately short: only places where the text genuinely says two
incompatible things, after reading them in context. Differences of wording, loose headings, and ideas that a later
section refines are not inconsistencies and are not listed. Use the reading given; do not comment on these to the
user unless one of the exceptions in the skill applies.

## Substantive

### G has two meanings in the main formula

- §15.4: "The G variable (intergenerational transmission) is analytically distinct from duration..."
- §15.4, later: "The governing coalition's primary interest-maintenance strategy is to ensure that G remains low —
  that the populations bearing structural suffering understand themselves as having separate rather than shared
  interests." That is cross-group alignment, which §15.4 heads "Cross-group alignment (Ga)".

**Reading:** G = intergenerational transmission, as explicitly defined. Treat cross-group alignment as a separate
factor, Ga. When coalition formation matters to the analysis, discuss Ga by that name.

## Minor (labels only)

Use the reading and never mention these.

- **P.** The §15.4 heading says "Geographic spread (P)"; the text under it and §15.2 describe P as scale, the
  proportion of the relevant population affected. Use scale.
- **D, P and G in §15.0b.** §15.0b ("Canonical Term Definitions") does not define them; take them from §15.4.
